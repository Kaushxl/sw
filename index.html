TRANSPARENT RULES

RENTAL POLICIES

Speed Limit (110 km/h)

Maximum permitted speed is 110 km/h. Any applicable fine or penalty for exceeding the permitted speed will be charged before return.

Extra Kilometres Rate

Additional kilometres beyond the allotted rental limit are charged at ₹10/km.

Booking Extension Policy

Extensions must be requested at least 6 hours before scheduled return time, subject to vehicle availability.

Weekend & Cancellation Rules

Hourly bookings are not available on weekends. Booking advance amounts are non-refundable for Friday, Saturday, or Sunday bookings.
